Outlayr is an expense tracker and budget app for Android, with iOS next, made by danestves LLC, the software studio of Daniel Esteves. It is not affiliated with Outlayr, Inc.

## One sentence, one entry

You log a purchase the way you would say it: type it, say it, or scan the receipt. Outlayr reads the amount, the merchant and the day, and files the entry.

## Budgets that look ahead

Each budget projects where this month’s pace ends, so a shortfall shows up while there is still time to change it.

## Money in 165 currencies

Accounts can hold any of 165 currencies. Every purchase is converted at the rate from its own day, not today’s.

## For everyone

Outlayr speaks English and Spanish. There is a free plan, there are no ads, and your data is never sold.

## How it is built

Expo and React Native on the phone and Convex behind it, local-first: an entry is saved on the device the moment you log it.

## Where to get it

On Google Play since 21 October 2026. The App Store version is coming soon.
